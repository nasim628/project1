About Us

Our Story

Established with a passion for authentic Persian cuisine and warm hospitality, Irani Hotel & Restaurant has been serving the community for over two decades. Our journey began with a simple vision: to bring the rich culinary traditions of Iran to our guests while providing a comfortable stay for travelers.

We take pride in using traditional recipes passed down through generations, combined with the freshest local ingredients to create dishes that delight the senses.
